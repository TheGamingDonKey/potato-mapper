# Lumen material correction — local 0.9.11

Shane identified that 0.9.10 was too white and did not match the approved Lumen frost study. The comparison found composition errors: the wide output pane inherited the denser sidebar tint, shell layers were too opaque toward their lower/right edges, and every section body added another pale fill absent from the reference.

The correction keeps the 0.8.8 geometry and existing controls. Lumen alone uses the study's diagonal header/output layering, thinner input tint, neutral section bodies and quiet section headings. A continuous cooler-left/warmer-right foundation replaces the pale diagonal wash. The selected surface retains its cobalt marker when another control has focus. Silver button faces and semantic icons retain their approved design. The first candidate's live output-status ink measured 4.37:1 over the darkest sampled backing; both primary and secondary label ink were adjusted, and the actual stopped-output status is now checked as well.

## Native frost evidence correction

The prior conclusion that every solid captured proof was necessarily a failed effect was wrong. The Windows capture sample's maintainer explains that window capture does not preserve effects sampling behind the window, including Acrylic: https://github.com/robmikh/Win32CaptureSample/issues/41#issuecomment-1024991777.

The isolated controller records State/StateChanged in an append-only log. It reported Active while foreground, Fallback when focus left, then Active on return. **Evidence correction, November 17:** the earlier questionnaire answer about a blurred patterned background was followed by Shane saying he did not understand what he was viewing. It is not reliable confirmation that native Acrylic works or that the design was accepted. Controller state and API success alone do not establish the visible effect. Shane now reports that the integrated app is opaque; the native desktop-blur requirement remains unresolved.

A second isolated proof compiles the actual desktop-glass adapter and selects documented DWMSBT_TRANSIENTWINDOW without replacing Qt's composition target. DWM accepted/read back material 3; a real button click registered and the OpenGL framebuffer remains valid, opaque and dark. Its live-screen blur check is pending. The same documented material selection is now integrated in the production adapter, with separate acceptance/readback diagnostics and a checked fallback. Accessibility preferences and other theme paths retain their previous behavior. No Windows App Runtime dependency is shipped and no desktop-capture pipeline is introduced. [Microsoft's material definition](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/dwmapi/ne-dwmapi-dwm_systembackdrop_type).
