# Creature and material design: independent challenge

15 October 2026. Research and design judgment only. Read `CONCEPTS.md` and `00-brief-and-candidates.md`; inspected `hollow-wood-concept-v1.png` through the image viewer. Four primary institutional sources were opened and read in this pass. No animation, app code, projection test or publication was performed.

## Finding

The strongest direction is a forest whose physical organization changes into a presence. The board currently falls back on familiar fantasy shorthand: antlered skull, glowing eyes, ragged cloaks, enormous moon and reptilian tree eye. Those motifs are not automatically unusable, but a shared brown-black palette does not make five distinct creatures. Originality cannot be established by this small source pass or by asserting that a generated image is new. A defensible creative contribution is a specific material rule and observable behavior that remains recognizable without the motif.

Keep Hollow Crown as the structural lead, provisionally. Make the other four earn different identities. The hero should be recognized by its changing silhouette before its eyes light; the procession by how it walks and loses a carrier; the crows by flight; the oracle by material opening; the wall by pressure and attachment to its surface.

## Shared material and silhouette rules

1. **One dominant physical analogy per entity.** Crown: load-bearing deadwood. Procession: walking bodies and suspended weight. Crows: feathered flight. Oracle: layered decay. Wall: surface tension and rupture. Do not cover every body in the same root spaghetti.
2. **Use large voids before fine texture.** A torso opening, separated legs or missing carrier can survive simplification. Hundreds of strands cannot be the essential identity. Check a flat silhouette and a reduced-size frame; this is a design screen, not proof of projector acceptance.
3. **Material direction must agree with motion.** Wood opens along authored grain; a wing folds at joints; a lantern lags its carrier. Unmotivated stretching weakens the illusion even when its texture is elaborate.
4. **A supernatural breach needs a normal rule first.** Show a grounded walk before the absent carrier; coherent flight before the impossible wing; still wood before the cavity attends to the viewer. One contradiction is stronger than constant unrelated wrongness.
5. **Avoid the repeated face.** One creature may look directly at the viewer. Other scenes should communicate awareness through coordinated orientation, path changes or contact. Reserve eyes, antlers and full frontal humanoids instead of using them as a collection-wide stamp.
6. **Keep the palette but vary the light's cause.** Procession light comes from lanterns, crow readability from the sky, oracle exposure from within a crack, Crown from broad backlight, wall from its near edge. Five amber glows on brown textures will feel less diverse than these causes suggest.

## Recommendation to the lead

Keep the five treatment mechanisms provisionally, but revise the visual brief before investing in detailed assets: give antlers exclusively to 01; remove the procession leader's antlers; thin the crow cloud; replace or explicitly fictionalize 04's reptile eye; conceal 05's complete stag. Treat the board as evidence of mood and problematic repetition, not evidence of completed creature design. If the first moving Crown proof cannot retain the torso void and stable crown structure through its turn, stop detail work and revise its correspondence rig. More roots, glow and fog would conceal that failure without solving it.
