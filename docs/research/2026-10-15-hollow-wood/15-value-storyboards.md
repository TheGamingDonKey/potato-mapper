## Reading this artifact

The values are dark `#182126`, middle `#667779`, light `#BEC9BF`, with amber `#D7A452`. Dark is the principal material, middle is secondary cover/support/ground, and light is open space. Amber marks lantern light, a fungal signal/cavity, or the one taut Weaver connection. Its use in a diagram does not imply an emissive material or a validated projection brightness.

These are deliberately simplified spatial proposals. A three-pose strip cannot establish continuous tree-to-body correspondence, a grounded gait, wood weight, causal propagation, a moving recognition hold, an invisible loop reset or a convincing landing. The Crown recovery frame proposes a broad occlusion zone; it does not prove a reset can pass unseen in motion. The Weaver strip begins with the creature already revealed so that contact, pull and folded recovery can be compared; it does not depict its earlier concealment/reveal. Its joints and contacts still need a moving rig test. The Roost keeps the feet fixed to make the support contradiction easy to inspect; its recovery isolates support restoration without depicting departure. The full departure/reconstruction choreography remains untested.
