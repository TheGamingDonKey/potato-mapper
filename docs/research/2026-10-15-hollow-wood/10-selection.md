## Rubric and uncertainty

Use the brief's weights unchanged:

**S = 0.20F + 0.20I + 0.20M + 0.15R + 0.15P + 0.10E.**

F = fit to dark enchanted forest / spectral creatures. I = specificity and distinctiveness of the proposed idea, not a claim of historical originality. M = authored motion potential, causal setup and meaningful resolution. R = likely survival of the essential act in large simplified forms; this is not a projector measurement. P = feasibility of making a convincing first loop within the inspected app and an original asset pipeline; higher means less unresolved production burden. E = relevance and directness of the available research/source/code support, not number of links and not rendered quality.

For each dimension, 0 means the premise contradicts the requirement or lacks an articulable route; 5 means plausible with major unresolved weaknesses; 8 means a strong proposal with specific remaining conditions; 10 means exceptionally strong on that *paper criterion*. A high fit score says nothing about achieved visual quality. Evidence is capped by the actual source access: nobody has shown the proposed motion or wall result. Multiple specialists repeating the same source do not create independent proof.

The CSV retains three decimal places solely so the weighted arithmetic can be reproduced; they are not three-decimal artistic precision. The table rounds to two. The CSV's plus/minus0.8–1.1 values are my subjective screening uncertainty, not statistical confidence intervals or measured error bars. Biggest uncertainties: actual creature performance, simplified recognition, moving joins, learning cost and physical contrast. Treat differences under roughly half a point as readily reversible, not a discovered ranking threshold.

## Why these five, and what would disqualify them

**01 — Hollow Crown: recognition of the environment as anatomy.** Best-supported first protagonist proof: large torso void, bent neck and dominant crown fork persist while a tree acquires agency. Reserve antlers to this scene. A severe creature still needs readable mass rather than luminous eye dots. Use a rooted turn first, not an entire walking stag. Fail it if the three landmarks drift, if the torso resets in plain view while only the head is hidden, or if the act is just two stills dissolving. Candidate18 gets the place if its simpler rigid bark construction communicates agency more convincingly.

**02 — Lantern Procession: normal relationships broken by absence.** Different social scale and repeated grounded motion establish a rule before one carrier/light separation. Three distinct load-bearing poses, uneven intervals and hand-to-light relationships matter more than more travelers. Keep one uncarried lantern as the sole breach; remove wrong-way shadow, bell explanation and a second face climax. Fail it if the bodies vanish into trunks before the viewer can establish their relationship to the lights, or if the survivors look like synchronized bobbing sprites.

**04 — Mycelium Oracle: macro material scrutiny.** The close material encounter varies scale from the other forest vistas. Three broad unequal routes lead to a physical cavity/lid event; suppress the network during the recognition hold. Prefer layered wood opening onto a reflective depth over a painted reptile eye, as09 proposes. This is a fictional forest organism colonized by fungi, not a scientific claim about a central fungal brain. Fail it if only a glowing circuit and eye decal remain after texture removal. A cavity must still visibly attend to something; eliminating a literal eye is not permission to eliminate the central event.

**17 — Weaver Above: tension and overhead occupation.** Promote from reserve. A few large silk arcs, real apparent anchor points, one initiating leg pair and a delayed canopy response give it a motion grammar absent from01–04. It can use edges without requiring a convincing deep hole in arbitrary plaster. The relevant03 treatment is one measured advance/tension release, not a full web simulation or many tiny strands. Fail it if a generic spider slides over unrelated scenery, all legs move together, or the broad clearing cannot retain its silhouette. Phobia is not an artistic score; a viewer must be able to follow tension, attachment and consequence even without fear of spiders.

**19 — Returning Roost: support disappears after weight arrives.** Promote the revised slot over03. A bird landing establishes a visible support; that support disappears while the bird holds position; broad missing gaps are restored on departure. The impossibility happens in negative space, which differs from a cloud changing shape. Keep at most three large crow/branch relationships, and use consistent crow landmarks from09. No moon, giant combined wing or additional face. Fail it if the gap is only a thin twig detail, the landing has no weight, or it reads as an ordinary perch loop. Missing supports must be evident within a single composition, rather than depending on memory of fine texture.

Portfolio coverage is therefore material recognition / social absence / macro scrutiny / overhead tension / impossible support. The actors occupy different apparent scales and locations. This is a proposed diversity advantage, not a claim that five brown forest scenes will automatically feel different. Their light causes and pacing must differ too.

## Strong rivals and genuine counterarguments

**1. Removing03 can sacrifice the clearest immediately legible sky event.** Its broad moon and large foreground crow may survive dark-wall conditions better than19's missing-support gap. It also has more developed native/motion/lighting treatment.19's high idea score is a judgment about a specific premise, not evidence of viewer comprehension. The fair decision test is two equally simple bird proofs: one directional eclipse/wing gesture versus one landing/support contradiction, each without detail or sound. If19's absence is missed, reinstate03. Do not combine both into one crow super-scene.

**2. Removing05 can sacrifice the strongest reason to use projection mapping.** A wall opening attached to a real panel does something a conventional woodland movie does not.17's anchors are a weaker form of site integration, and its silhouette can become another familiar Halloween overlay. In a known installation,05 could outrank either17 or19 even though it has lower generic portability. Compare an entity-free seam/closure proof against a plain framed forest control on one actual target panel. If attachment survives the intended viewing region, promote05. The current information does not justify paying its geometry/edge-authoring cost for every neutral surface.

**3. Keeping01 over18 may reward research attention and iconic familiarity.**18 is less dependent on antlers or faces, has a very specific three-contact action and can expose an empty interior. Its raw score is essentially tied with17 and19. I exclude it because01 already occupies deadwood-to-agent transformation, not because it is weak. In a broader collection, it deserves an alternative hero proof: three stiff contacts, one sideways step, concealed collapse. If the bark seams can act rigidly and the turn in01 fails, replace01; do not add18 as a second near-identical structural reveal.

**4. Two promoted challengers lack equal development and generated-board support.** Novelty can look cheap in prose because the difficult joints and resets have not been built.17 needs articulated leg contacts and19 needs landings/weight shifts. Their feasibility/evidence marks are deliberately lower than their fit/idea marks, but that penalty could still be insufficient. A conservative production shortlist01/02/03/04/05 is defensible for comparative animatics; it is not automatically the most diverse finished collection. My changed shortlist is a proposal to test, not an order to discard the root's candidates.

**The owl rival must be acknowledged fairly.** Specialist01's *Hollow That Breathes* is a substantial owl/woodland-opening treatment;02 also proposes a giant articulated bird. It is a serious single-creature alternative with potentially broad wing readability and fewer crowd agents than03. There is no owl ID among the agreed twenty. Account for it as a **one-for-one treatment revision of05**, replacing the antlered watcher with a partial spectral owl, while the seam remains the defining act. It is not candidate21 and cannot inherit05's score as though an owl rig and wing/bark transformation were free. The cleanest comparison retains a partial owl watching through a closing seam; adding a full wing-to-bark metamorphosis creates a second major act. If the large bird is chosen instead as the central event, rescore that revised candidate openly. Rejecting owls simply because the first board already shows crows would be circular.

## Why the remaining slots do not take a first-five place

06 is valuable structure but duplicates01's root alignment.07 has a specific behavioral contradiction but the least robust broad-shape readability; it needs a standalone shadow test before becoming an entire scene.08 offers depth but depends on water/reflection geography and a dark field that projection cannot physically impose.09 is a readable broad form, yet a symmetrical moth opening can remain a decorative fantasy emblem unless it acts consequentially.

10 has a strong hanging silhouette, but its repeated heartbeat is a cycle without a sufficiently clear state change.11's portrait transformation needs more face performance and fine recognition while competing with04's material encounter.12 has excellent motion potential and predatory agency; it pays for gait, weight, ash continuity and reset at once. It is a strong later ambitious reserve, not evidence that difficult creature work should never be attempted.

13 offers a good directional reversal and seasonal interlude, but its spectral payoff arrives late.14's visible resonance is insufficiently specific about why a distant figure reacts when muted; its pulse also duplicates04.15's poisonous beauty is a useful texture/material alternative but gives up too much ominous agency.16 has specific wrongness but divides attention across matched bodies and reflected space.20 is a useful warm departure/coda, yet fire/sparks-to-spirits is the most familiar and gentlest ending in this group; a coda should not take a hero slot merely to round out five.

These are reasons to defer a particular first collection, not claims of universally bad ideas.

## What would settle this selection

Use equally sized low-detail moving proofs, with no title explaining the trick. Compare01 against18 first if the Crown rig is fragile; compare19 against03 for the bird place; compare17 against a panel-specific05 seam proof where there is an actual target surface. The macro04 and social02 slots are valuable only while their events remain distinct and legible.

Apply08's timing advice: duration follows the action, not a mandatory24-second template. Preserve silent setup, a protected recognition hold, one consequence and a fully accounted reset. A moving proof must show all visible anatomy, light and foliage crossing the join, not just matching endpoint stills. Actual mapper playback and physical projector observation remain later separate evidence.

**No implementation, new app capability, asset licence, seamless decoder behavior, fear response, final quality score or projector pass follows from this paper selection.** The useful result is a five-place recommendation with explicit rivals and conditions under which it should change.
