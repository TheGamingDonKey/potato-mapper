## Forest of Shadows benchmark

Opened the official [Moment Factory page](https://momentfactory.com/products/forest-of-shadows) in Chrome; its embedded official YouTube promo is [38 seconds long](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kuTgGnaH89g). Clicked through its playback controls and inspected screenshots at the displayed 0:03, approximately 0:18 and end title. The observed 0:03 shot uses high-contrast geometric mapping with tree context. The 0:18 shot combines red light, atmospheric haze, small blue lights, a pram and dolls among an outdoor path. The end/title frame shows viewers facing a huge circular projected face. These selected frames show scale, staged light and an environment; they do **not** prove the timing, fear, seamlessness, or laptop feasibility of the proposed Hollow Wood loops. The clip played to completion between inspections; that is not equivalent to watching every frame.

The artist's documented forest/scare direction supports reference relevance, not a claim that its show contains our proposed stag, root oracle or crow transformation. Those are original concepts. The artwork and promo were not downloaded or proposed for redistribution.
