# App references for Windows Liquid Glass 1

Research proposal, 31 October 2026. This is a third complete interface theme alongside Classic 1 and Material. Preserve the familiar 0.8.8 arrangement, labelled actions, projector controls, feature locations and black mapping canvas. The references inform the finish of existing controls; their layouts are not migration proposals.

## Evidence and selection

Primary product, release and design pages were read, and the official images linked below were displayed in a browser without downloading media. These are visual observations, not hands-on app or accessibility tests. “Published” distinguishes the maker's explanation from what an image establishes. Device montages and promotional shading limit conclusions about small text, true opacity and real backdrop behavior. None establishes an objectively best interface.

| Reference | Strongest contribution | Adoption boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Sketch Copenhagen, 2025 | Dense creative controls with selective glass | Keep Potato Mapper's existing fixed panels and toolbars |
| Craft OS26, 2025 | Cohesive chrome and restrained rounding | Borrow finish, not document cards or mobile navigation |
| Pixelmator Pro, current Apple page | Dark optical panels around rich content | Keep the mapper canvas and projected image independent |
| Final Cut Pro, current Apple guide | Stable neutral chrome under dense creative work | Its timeline and browser are not mapper features |
| Raycast Tahoe, 2025 | Glass shell, clear row selection, restrained decoration | A launcher has far fewer controls than this editor |
| Linear refresh, March 2026 | Lower saturation and quiet hierarchy | Do not copy extremely dim inactive labels |
| PowerToys Command Palette, current docs | Real Windows backdrop and readable foreground layers | Wallpaper-colored Acrylic needs a bounded tint |

### Sketch Copenhagen

The [19 November 2025 design tour](https://www.sketch.com/blog/a-tour-of-copenhagen/) is unusually relevant: Sketch tried floating/glass sidebars, then chose fixed custom sidebars as less distracting for a canvas tool. Its published glass locations include the toolbar, minimap and notifications. The [viewed inspector image](https://sktch.b-cdn.net/assets/blog/2025-11/prototyping-panel.png?expires=1793378697&quality=95&sharpen=1&token=KbH0zKt7kCsxKItSQbnj2mLP9v90qrrsVjP0jMmzCAY&width=1920) shows consistent pale field beds, compact segmented controls and a clear label/value rhythm. That supports stable control interiors within a visibly optical theme. It does not justify relocating Potato Mapper's inspector or replacing its labelled toolbar. The CDN image URL is signed; return to the source page if it expires.

### Craft

Craft's [15 September 2025 release](https://www.craft.do/blog/craft-update-3-2-6) describes softer rounding and glass where it improves clarity while preserving its core identity. The [viewed Mac/iPad/iPhone montage](https://www.craft.do/images/content/blog/craft-update-3-2-6/image-1.jpg) shows light translucent chrome, a restrained toolbar pill, quiet sidebar grouping and content cards carrying most color. Reusable lesson: repeat one material language across the application and let selected content supply stronger color. This image is predominantly light mode; it supplies no evidence for a specific dark palette or Windows rendering technique.

### Pixelmator Pro

The [current Apple product page](https://www.apple.com/pixelmator-pro/) and [viewed device hero](https://www.apple.com/v/pixelmator-pro/a/images/overview/welcome/hero_endframe__ecdif99jtfsm_large.jpg) show dark edge panels around a colorful image, subtle luminous panel boundaries and compact controls. The picture's purple belongs to the artwork and its apparent reflections; it is not a reason to choose a purple editor theme. Borrow the separation between the content and its control surfaces, plus the coherent dark optical perimeter. The montage is too small to establish font sizes or exact transparency. Its touch-oriented iPad inspector is not a desktop layout prescription.

### Final Cut Pro

The [current interface guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/final-cut-pro/final-cut-pro-interface-ver92bd100a/mac) provides a [viewed full-window screenshot](https://help.apple.com/assets/6A29F5537442EFE69C0CD4F3/6A29F558FEEBDB01270C8EF2/en_US/48d8c05bdce354cafaec42b8df7874b9.png). Charcoal regions, small neutral separators and consistent controls keep dense media, video and timeline areas distinguishable. Strong color largely communicates content or clip roles. The [current product hero](https://www.apple.com/v/final-cut-pro/w/images/overview/welcome/hero_endframe__r3cnyk748duq_large.jpg) reinforces that quiet perimeter. The guide image is a functional hierarchy reference, not proof that every current Final Cut surface uses Liquid Glass. For Mapper, gloss should enrich the chrome without changing projected color or darkening the work itself.

### Raycast

The [15 September 2025 Tahoe release](https://www.raycast.com/changelog/1-103-0) says Liquid Glass controls were added to AI Chat. Its [viewed release artwork](https://releases.raycast.com/user-attachments/d5bc1c3b-6daa-4689-b398-b12729a8cffc) actually depicts the launcher: smoked translucency, a thin luminous perimeter, one broad selected row and distinct shortcut badges. The AI Chat statement is a release claim, not something verified in that image. Useful transfer: popup/menu selection should read as a coherent illuminated surface, with crisp labels and secondary shortcut text, rather than a bright rectangular color patch.

### Linear

Linear's [12 March 2026 design account](https://linear.app/now/behind-the-latest-design-refresh) explains a move from cool bluish neutrals toward crisper, warmer, less saturated gray. Its [viewed before/after sidebar](https://webassets.linear.app/images/ornj730p/production/b6d6be14c96978b10553cfb9205be1065087e793-3904x2720.png?auto=format&dpr=2&q=95) shows refined icons, muted inactive navigation and one quiet selected pill. The image has promotional shading; its apparent darkness should not become a contrast target. Borrow the controlled palette and consistent hierarchy. Keep Mapper's operational labels easier to read, and express glass through edges, depth and highlights instead of adding blue to every background.

### PowerToys Command Palette

Microsoft's [current overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/powertoys/command-palette/overview), updated 21 October 2026, links the [viewed animated demonstration](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/powertoys/images/command-palette/cmdpal-overview1.gif). Displayed frames showed blurred teal/blue wallpaper behind a dark shell, a brighter selected row, a side detail pane and stable bottom commands. [Settings documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/powertoys/command-palette/settings) identifies Acrylic as the default and offers other backdrops. This is strong Windows-specific evidence for layered materials, but also shows why uncontrolled wallpaper influence could overwhelm a neutral creative theme. Preserve readable beds and constrain saturation.

## Palette alternatives

These are proposed tokens, not sampled brand colors. Canvas black remains `#000000` in every option.

| Direction | Shell / glass bed | Pearl / primary text | Accent | Tradeoff |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Smoke and Jade — recommended** | `#171A19` / `#272A29` | `#E8EEE8` / `#F2F3EF` | `#8FD0B2` | Near-neutral glass with a distinct, quiet active state |
| Graphite and Champagne | `#1C1B19` / `#2B2925` | `#EEEAE0` / `#F4F1E9` | `#C9BE98` | Warmer and elegant; excess warmth risks muddy brown |
| Carbon and Seafoam | `#171A1B` / `#272D2E` | `#E8EFEF` / `#F0F4F3` | `#94CED0` | Clean cooler option; easier to drift back into blue chrome |

Smoke and Jade offers the clearest departure from the existing blue/purple/orange impression while retaining dark creative-tool restraint. Use secondary text `#AFB8B3`, recessed fields `#1D2220`, and hover/upper glass limit around `#3A3F3C`. Calculated opaque sRGB contrast is approximately 13.0:1 for primary text and 7.1:1 for secondary text on `#272A29`; even on `#3A3F3C`, approximately 9.6:1 and 5.3:1. Dark `#11241C` labels on jade reach approximately 9.2:1. Real composited states still require measurement.

## Proposed whole-app composition

Apply a common optical stack throughout the existing chrome: smoked depth, a broad restrained pearl sheen, a narrow brighter upper/side rim, a darker lower edge and a soft contact shadow. Highlights should follow curved boundaries and fade across the surface. This must visibly read as polished glass across the app, beyond a recolored Material theme; solid gradients alone do not prove refraction.

Keep menu and toolbar bands in place, carrying a continuous smoked base with distinct glossy button faces. Treat the projector band consistently, reserving strong state color for existing output/blackout meaning. The left sidebar remains fixed: its outer shell receives glass depth, existing section boundaries receive fine pearl separators, and fields sit on stable recessed beds. Sliders, checkboxes, tabs, spin controls and selected rows share the same edge light and jade active language. Menus, tooltips, dialogs and scrollbars continue that family instead of reverting to unrelated flat gray.

Retain existing typography metrics and control positions; use normal and semibold weights to distinguish labels, headings and values. Keep keyboard focus visibly distinct from selection, and preserve semantic warning/error colors. Under reduced transparency, retain the sheen, rim and depth with a stable darker bed. All effects stay in editor chrome: no blur, tint, reflection, shadow or desktop sampling may alter the black mapping canvas or projector output. Visual acceptance must include the actual full app with menus, dialogs and dense controls open, rather than judging a single attractive popup.
